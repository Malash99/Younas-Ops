  🔄 Key Technical Achievement: Coordinate Frame Transformation

  The Critical Problem Solved:
  # ❌ WRONG: Direct use of Qualisys world poses
  world_delta = world_pose_t1 - world_pose_t0  # Wrong coordinate frame!

  # ✅ CORRECT: Transform to camera coordinate frame
  delta_camera = transform_world_to_camera(world_pose_t1, world_pose_t0)
  # Result: [dx, dy, dz, droll, dpitch, dyaw] in camera frame

  Why This Matters:
  - Your model predicts motion in camera coordinates (X=right, Y=down, Z=forward)
  - Qualisys gives poses in world coordinates (X=north, Y=east, Z=down)
  - Without transformation, the model learns incorrect motion patterns
  - Our pipeline ensures proper camera-relative learning

  🎯 Ready-to-Use Training Data

  CSV Schema (key columns):
  - delta_x, delta_y, delta_z - Translation deltas in camera frame
  - delta_roll, delta_pitch, delta_yaw - Rotation deltas in camera frame
  - cam0_path through cam4_path - Multi-camera image paths
  - accel_x/y/z, gyro_x/y/z - IMU sensor data
  - pressure - Depth/barometer readings
  - thrust_ch0 through thrust_chN - Motor control commands
